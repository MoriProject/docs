## 2. Definitions

- **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ("Data Subject").
- **"Controller"** means the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data.
- **"Processor"** means the entity that processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.
- **"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether automated or not, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, alignment, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

## 7. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Processing temporary files | Deleted immediately after processing |
| Pending order files | 12 hours |
| Order records | 24 hours |
| Completed order files | 30 days |
| API request logs | 90 days |
| Account information | Duration of account + 30 days after deletion |
| Billing records | As required by law (up to 5 years) |

Upon expiration of the applicable retention period, Personal Data shall be securely deleted or anonymized.

**Deletion Methods:**
- **Electronic files (databases, file storage):** Deleted using cryptographic erasure or overwrite methods that render data unrecoverable.
- **Logs:** Automatically purged after the retention window expires.
- **Paper records (if any):** Shredded or incinerated in compliance with applicable standards.

**Retention Justification:** Each retention period is determined based on the minimum period necessary for the stated purpose: temporary files are deleted immediately as they serve no purpose beyond processing; order records and files are retained for the minimum period needed for customer support and dispute resolution; logs are retained for 90 days to support security monitoring and incident investigation; billing records are retained as required by tax and accounting laws.

## 13. Privacy by Design

MORI adheres to the principles of Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default. This means that data protection considerations are embedded into the design and architecture of the Service from inception, and that the default settings provide the highest level of privacy protection. Specifically: (a) we minimize data collection to what is strictly necessary; (b) we pseudonymize and encrypt data where feasible; (c) we conduct internal privacy impact assessments for new features; (d) we limit data access to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
