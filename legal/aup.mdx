This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") is part of the BizMORI Terms of Service between **MORI SOLUTION, Inc.** ("MORI") and Customer. It defines the permitted and prohibited uses of the BizMORI service ("Service").

---

## 1. General Principles

The BizMORI service is designed to protect images from unauthorized AI training and usage through digital watermark embedding and detection. You shall use the Service lawfully, ethically, and in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses

The Service may be used for:
- Embedding protective watermarks into images you own or have authorization to process
- Detecting watermarks in images for verification purposes
- Integrating watermark protection into your products, platforms, or workflows via the API
- Protecting copyrighted visual content from unauthorized AI training or reproduction

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses

You shall not use the Service to:

### 3.1 Illegal Activities
- Process images in violation of Applicable Law
- Facilitate copyright infringement, identity theft, or fraud
- Process content that constitutes child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — such content will be reported to authorities
- Use the Service for spamming, phishing, or any form of unsolicited bulk communications
- Process content that violates the Korean Information and Communications Network Act (정보통신망법) Article 44-7, including obscene material, defamatory content, or content that infringes privacy

### 3.2 Unauthorized Content
- Process images you do not own or do not have the right to modify
- Remove, alter, or circumvent watermarks placed by third parties for copyright protection
- Use detection features to strip or reverse-engineer third-party watermarks
- Process content in violation of the DMCA (17 U.S.C. §1201-1202), including circumvention of copyright management information
- Process content in violation of the Korean Copyright Act (저작권법) Article 103

### 3.3 Service Abuse
- Circumvent or attempt to circumvent rate limits, quotas, or access controls
- Perform vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, or security testing without prior written authorization from MORI
- Use automated means to create multiple free accounts to circumvent plan limits
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract the watermarking algorithms
- Share, publish, or embed API Keys in client-side code, public repositories, or insecure locations

### 3.4 AI Training and Competitive Use
- Use the Service, its outputs, or any data derived therefrom to train, develop, or improve any machine learning or artificial intelligence model
- Use the Service for the purpose of benchmarking, competitive analysis, or evaluating the Service against competing products, unless MORI provides prior written consent
- Systematically collect, scrape, or aggregate outputs from the Service for purposes other than the Customer's ordinary business use of watermark protection

### 3.5 Automated Access
- Deploy automated bots, scripts, or agents that interact with the Service in a manner that exceeds reasonable API usage patterns or degrades service quality for other customers
- Use the Service as a back-end for automated pipelines without appropriate rate limiting and error handling on the Customer's side
- Automated access shall comply with the rate limits specified in the Customer's Plan and shall implement exponential backoff for retry logic

### 3.6 Harmful Content
- Process images containing malware or malicious code
- Upload content specifically designed to attack or degrade the Service
- Submit excessively large or malformed files intended to exhaust system resources

### 3.7 Resale Without Authorization
- Resell, redistribute, or provide access to the Service to third parties as a standalone offering without MORI's prior written consent
- White-label the Service without an appropriate agreement

## 4. Content Standards

While MORI does not actively monitor the content of uploaded images, we reserve the right to take action if we become aware that Customer Data violates this AUP. Specifically:

- **CSAM:** Any detection or report of CSAM will result in immediate account termination and reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline and/or relevant law enforcement authorities in the applicable jurisdiction.
- **Sanctioned content:** Content that violates trade sanctions or export controls is prohibited.
- **EU Illegal Content (DSA).** To the extent the EU Digital Services Act applies, content that is illegal under EU or EU Member State law (including but not limited to terrorist content, illegal hate speech, and illegal commercial practices) is prohibited. MORI provides a notice-and-action mechanism in accordance with DSA Article 16 — reports may be submitted to mori@mori-corp.io with "DSA Report" in the subject line.
- **Korean Illegal Content.** Content that violates Korean law, including the Information and Communications Network Act and the Copyright Act, is prohibited. MORI may take interim measures (임시조치) as required by the Act.

## 6. Enforcement

### 6.1 Actions
MORI may take the following actions for AUP violations:

1. **Warning:** Written notice of the violation with a request to cease the activity.
2. **Throttling:** Temporary reduction of rate limits.
3. **Suspension:** Temporary suspension of API access pending investigation.
4. **Termination:** Permanent termination of the account for severe or repeated violations.

### 6.2 Process
- For non-critical violations, MORI shall provide written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure (typically 5 business days).
- For critical violations (illegal content, active security threats), MORI may suspend access immediately without prior notice.
- Customer may appeal enforcement decisions by contacting mori@mori-corp.io within 10 business days.

### 6.3 DSA Compliance (Statement of Reasons)
In compliance with the EU Digital Services Act Article 17, when MORI restricts or terminates access based on content violations, MORI shall provide the affected Customer with: (a) a clear and specific statement of the reasons for the restriction; (b) reference to the specific legal or contractual ground relied upon; (c) information about available redress mechanisms, including internal complaint handling. MORI maintains an internal complaint-handling system in accordance with DSA Article 20; complaints may be submitted to mori@mori-corp.io with "DSA Complaint" in the subject line.

### 6.4 Responsible AI Use
Customers shall use the Service in a manner consistent with responsible AI principles. This includes: (a) ensuring transparency about the use of AI watermarking in Customer's products; (b) not using the Service to create misleading or deceptive content; (c) complying with applicable AI regulations, including the EU AI Act.

### 6.5 Vulnerability Disclosure
MORI welcomes responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities. If you discover a vulnerability in the Service, please report it to mori@mori-corp.io with "Security Vulnerability" in the subject line. MORI will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and work to address verified vulnerabilities promptly. MORI will not take legal action against individuals who report vulnerabilities in good faith and in compliance with this policy.
