# BizMORI Sub-processors List

**Last Updated:** November 1, 2025

---

This page lists the third-party Sub-processors engaged by MORI SOLUTION, Inc. and MORI Corp. to provide the BizMORI service. Sub-processors process Personal Data on behalf of MORI in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

MORI will update this list as Sub-processors are added or removed. Customers will be notified of material changes at least 30 days in advance via email, in accordance with Section 6.3 of the DPA.

---

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Location | Data Processed |
|---------------|---------|----------|----------------|
| **MORI Corp.** (주식회사 모리) | Service operations, infrastructure management, technical support | South Korea | All Customer Data processed through the Service |

---

## Security and Compliance

All Sub-processors are contractually bound to maintain data protection obligations no less protective than those in the BizMORI DPA, including:

- **Encryption:** Data encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent)
- **Access Control:** Role-based access control and principle of least privilege
- **Security Monitoring:** Continuous monitoring and incident response procedures
- **Compliance:** Adherence to applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, PIPA)
- **Audits:** Subject to security audits and compliance verification

## International Data Transfers

For information about cross-border data transfers to Sub-processors, please refer to the [Cross-Border Data Transfer Addendum](docs.bizmori.com/legal/cross-border-transfer).
